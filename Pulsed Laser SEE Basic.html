Pulsed-laser single event effects

Laser testing mimics ion strikes: a focused picosecond pulse creates electron-hole pairs in silicon. Tune the beam, then fire it at a digital SRAM array or an analog op-amp / comparator and explore the graphs (hover for readouts, tap a sweep graph to set the energy).

Transient vs. laser energy

A laser-induced analog single-event transient is the charge pulse filtered by the circuit's bandwidth: fast amplifiers pass a tall, narrow spike (clipped at the rails); slow ones give a small, long ripple. Time axis is logarithmic.
